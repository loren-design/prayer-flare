WHAT IS A WATCHMAN?

Ancient calling. Modern mission.

In ancient times, watchmen stood on city walls through the night. They watched for danger. They sounded the alarm. They never slept when others needed protection.

Today, people are fighting battles alone — addiction, anxiety, depression, spiritual attack. They're reaching out in their darkest moments, and they need someone standing in the gap.

"I looked for someone among them who would build up the wall and stand before me in the gap on behalf of the land..."

— Ezekiel 22:30

That's where you come in. Prayer Flare Watchman receive real-time prayer requests from people in crisis. You pray. You intercede. You post prayers of encouragement to their private prayer wall. You stand when they can't.

📱
