Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we protect it.

Last updated: January 2025

🛡️ The Short Version

  • ✅ We don't collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • ✅ We don't track you across websites
  • ✅ We don't sell or share any data with third parties
  • ✅ Your prayer requests are completely anonymous
  • ✅ We only store what's needed to connect you with prayer warriors

What We Collect

When you send a prayer flare, we store only two pieces of information:

Your name or email ❌ Never collected
Your IP address ❌ Not stored
Your zip code ⚡ Stored anonymously
Prayer category ⚡ Stored anonymously
Cookies or trackers ❌ None
Account required ❌ No accounts

That's it. We use your zip code to notify local prayer warriors (if available) and the category to help them pray specifically. There is no way to trace a prayer request back to you.

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used for one purpose only: connecting you with prayer warriors.

When you send a flare:

1. Prayer warriors in your zip code area are notified
2. They see only the category (e.g., "Anxiety") and general location
3. They have no way to identify or contact you directly

We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Data Storage & Security

Prayer requests are stored securely and are automatically deleted after 30 days. We use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit.

Your prayer wall (if you choose to view it) uses a randomly generated access token — not your personal information — to give you access.

Third Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. Period.

We use Supabase for our database, which is a trusted, secure platform. No other third parties have access to any prayer request data.

Children's Privacy

Prayer Flare is available to users of all ages. Because we don't collect any personal information, there is no special risk to minors using the service.

Your Rights

Because we don't collect personal information, there's nothing to delete, correct, or export. Your prayer requests are anonymous from the moment you send them.

If you have any questions about your privacy, we're happy to answer them.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how we handle data (which would be rare), we'll update this page and note the date at the top.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, reach out at
privacy@prayerflare.com
