Our Mission

Prayer Flare exists for one reason: to make sure no one faces their darkest moments alone.

When temptation hits at 2am. When anxiety won't let go. When the craving screams louder than your willpower. When you're not sure you can make it through the next hour — that's when you need someone standing with you.

We're not counselors. We're not a hotline. We're a community of believers who have made a simple commitment: when a flare goes up, we pray. Immediately. Anonymously. Relentlessly.

"Again, truly I tell you that if two of you on earth agree about anything they ask for, it will be done for them by my Father in heaven. For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them."

— Matthew 18:19-20

Prayer Warriors. Always Ready. Always Listening.

⚡

How It Works

You send up a flare. Just your zip code and what you're facing — that's it. No account. No personal information. Completely anonymous.

Within moments, prayer warriors in your area (and around the world) are notified. They don't know who you are. They just know someone needs prayer. And they pray.

Meanwhile, we guide you through a "Right Now Prayer" — something to help you connect with God in that exact moment. Because sometimes you need words when you can't find your own.

The Watchmen

Our prayer warriors call themselves "Watchmen" — inspired by the ancient guards who stood on city walls, watching for danger and calling out warnings in the night.

Today's Watchmen stand guard in a different way. They watch for flares. They intercede for strangers. They stand in the gap for people they'll never meet — because that's what the body of Christ does.

Want to become a Watchman? If you feel called to pray for others in their moments of crisis, we need you on the wall.
