The Watchman

In ancient times, watchmen stood on city walls through the night. They watched for danger. They called out warnings. They never left their post.

Today's Watchman stand guard in a different way. They watch for flares. They intercede for strangers. They stand in the gap for people they'll never meet.

Because that's what the body of Christ does.
